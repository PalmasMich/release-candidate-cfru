# Cagliari Preview 0.1 — private ROM smoke checklist

Use this only after a fresh private build completes successfully. The ROM itself must stay local and must not be committed or distributed.

## Gate A — opening identity

1. Start a new game.
2. Confirm the opening says **Release Candidate** and presents the speaker as the **Delivery Lead**.
3. Confirm the naming prompts use **YOUR ID?** and **TEAMMATE ID?**.
4. Walk toward the grass before receiving a partner. Confirm the interruption is Delivery/field-test themed rather than vanilla Oak exposition.
5. Confirm the starting town presentation reads **CAGLIARI** and the lab exterior reads **DELIVERY HUB - CAGLIARI**.

## Gate B — Delivery Hub and starter

6. Enter the Delivery Hub and confirm the three choices are framed as release candidates/resources.
7. Choose **Tartrek** for the canonical smoke path.
8. Confirm Tartrek is Grass/Ground and starts with usable moves; the battle menu must not be empty.
9. Confirm Tartrek front/back/name presentation is intact in battle.
10. Optional regression pass: separately verify Frobyte and Emberfox occupy the other two original starter slots.

## Gate C — KPI rival

11. Trigger the starter battle.
12. Confirm the rival uses the expected counter starter and the surrounding dialogue references KPI/benchmark/velocity rather than vanilla rival copy.
13. Win or lose once and confirm the result/exit dialogue remains corporate-themed.

## Gate D — Port Link first custom encounter

14. Leave Cagliari toward the former Route 1.
15. Confirm the route sign reads **PORT LINK / CAGLIARI - MARINA PORTO**.
16. Confirm the Delivery NPC explicitly mentions **Mistrillo** and field testing.
17. Enter the first reachable grass and obtain several encounters. The runtime table contract is 60% Mistrillo / 25% Wingull / 15% Meowth; the first random roll is not required to be Mistrillo.
18. When Mistrillo appears, confirm its custom name/sprite render and the battle remains playable.

## Gate E — Marina teaser

19. Reach Marina Porto.
20. Confirm the city identity is no longer Viridian and the sign/area presents **DEPLOY BLOCKED - CHECK SCOPE** / deploy-gate flavor.

## Failure capture

For any failure, record: gate + step number, screenshot, what was expected, what actually happened, and whether the game crashed or remained controllable. Do not continue after a reproducible crash; keep the save/ROM private and report the exact step.
